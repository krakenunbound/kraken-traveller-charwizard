# Changelog

## 1.0-beta3 (2026-10-23)

- **Fix:** the wizard's two settings in the Options window (Traveller Wizard group) had blank names. They now read **Players may reroll characteristics** and **Rules printing (education and anagathics values)**. The settings themselves worked all along; only their labels were missing.

## 1.0-beta2 (2026-10-22)

Fixes from forum feedback (thanks for the careful review against the Core Rulebook Update 2022).

- **Connections rule:** taking a Connection skill no longer lowers your other specialties. Example: with Electronics (computers) 4, a Connection in Electronics (sensors) now gives sensors 1 and leaves computers at 4. If the Connection would take the chosen skill above 3, or gives no new level, it is refused and stays free for another choice.
- **New option, Rules printing:** *Core Rulebook (2016)* (default) or *Core Rulebook Update 2022*. The 2022 setting uses the Update's values: University entry EDU 6+, graduation INT 6+, honours 10+, EDU +1 on graduation (on top of the +1 on entry); Army/Marine/Navy academy entry END 7+/END 8+/INT 8+, academy graduation INT 7+; anagathics 1D x Cr25,000 a term. All roll buttons and texts follow the option.
- **Unfinished Travellers are kept:** closing the wizard no longer loses the build; reopening it carries on where you were. **Start over** on the Characteristics page (click twice to confirm) begins a new Traveller. The build still clears on Create Character or a reload of FGU.

## 1.0-beta1 (2026-10-20)

First public release.

- Full Mongoose Traveller 2E (Core Rulebook) creation in Fantasy Grounds Unity on the MGT2 ruleset: characteristics, species, background skills, career terms (education, qualification, Draft, Drifter, basic training, skills, survival, events, mishaps, life and unusual events, commissions, advancement, ranks, ageing, anagathics, injuries, prison and parole, psionics and the Psion career), mustering out, benefits, Connections, skill packages and the finished sheet.
- All 14 careers with three assignments each.
- Gear benefits chosen from the loaded books' items within each benefit's cost and Tech Level limit; medical care with employer cover.
- Psionic talents are added through the ruleset, so their powers appear on the sheet.
- Every roll shown in chat with its target and DM.
- Tested in FGU by building Travellers on screen and with thousands of simulated careers.
